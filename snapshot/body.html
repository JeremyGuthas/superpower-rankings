Where this week's ranks came from

    How it works

    Every Tuesday, once the week's power rankings have been published, the aggregator finds each outlet's new article, reads all 32 teams out of it, and averages the ranks. A team's Superpower Score is that average — lower is better. Ties break on median rank, then on the team's single best ranking.

    A source only counts when a complete, duplicate-free 1–32 ranking can be read out of its article. Anything short of that is left out of the average for the week and listed above as a miss, so the score is never built on a half-parsed list.
